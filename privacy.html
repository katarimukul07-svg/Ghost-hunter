Echo Steps Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Echo Steps does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your pilot name, preferences, unlocked cosmetics, coin balance, and best score locally on your device. This information is used only to preserve game progress and is not sent to the developer.

Permissions and third parties

The release uses device lifecycle, status-bar, and haptic capabilities. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or tracking permission. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Game Center (iOS)

On iPhone and iPad, the game uses Apple Game Center for its leaderboard. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best number of rounds is submitted to Apple and shown with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive your Game Center account details. You can turn Game Center off in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases (iOS)

Optional coin packs are sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment details. The game records only which purchases have already been credited, on your device, so coins are not added twice.

The optional Share button opens your device's standard share interface. The game does not receive information about where or whether you share.

Children

Because the game does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Web version

The web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy terms. The game itself does not send analytics or advertising data.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before any release adds data collection, advertising, analytics, or accounts. For privacy or support questions, use the Echo Steps support page.
