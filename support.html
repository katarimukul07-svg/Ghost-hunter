Echo Steps Support

For help with Echo Steps: Garbage Collector, report a problem through the project's GitHub issue tracker.

Before reporting a problem

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, the round where the problem occurred, and the steps that reproduce it. Do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data.

Progress and deletion

Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes saved progress. The developer cannot recover locally deleted progress.
